Our Story

Crafting Beautiful Interiors in Chennai

At InEx Designs Interiors, we believe that every space has a unique potential waiting to be unlocked. Based in Madhavaram, Chennai, we bring high-end interior design and quality craftsmanship to homes and businesses across the city.

From elegant wooden partitions and aluminium work to PVC shelves, lofts, kitchens, and full home makeovers — our team delivers with professionalism, punctuality, and a passion for perfection.
